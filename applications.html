Commercial Agriculture

ArableONE™ is not a fertilizer. It is a biological activator — a single seasonal application that stimulates the native microorganisms and fungi already present in your soil to proliferate at a dramatically faster rate. The result is a deeper, stronger root zone that gives your crops greater access to water and nutrients than conventional fertilizer programs ever could.

Documented across avocados, strawberries, alfalfa, sugarcane, corn, and dozens of other crop types — on some of the most challenging soils in the world.

  • Max 2 quarts per acre — works with all irrigation systems
  • Compatible with existing fertility and IPM programs
  • Improves severely depleted and chemically damaged soils
  • No special equipment, no new programs, no second application
  • Lab analysis available — Wallace Laboratories, EPA 3051A certified
