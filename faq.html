Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need
to Know.

How does ArableONE™ work? +

ArableONE™ increases the efficiency with which plants absorb nutrients by creating a soil environment that enhances root zone development. This is accomplished through fostering increased growth of beneficial microorganisms and fungi which occur naturally in soil.

Is this a fertilizer? +

No. ArableONE™ does not add synthetic nutrients. It activates the native microorganisms already in your soil — stimulating them to improve root depth, water retention, and nutrient uptake. Fertilizers feed the plant externally. ArableONE™ rebuilds the soil system that feeds the plant naturally.

How does it reduce water use? +

Deeper, fuller root systems give plants greater access to water and nutrients. This reduces the damaging effects of drought, heat, frost, and nutrient deficiencies — meaning your existing water supply goes further. Documented reductions of up to 50% in irrigation demand.

How much do I need to apply? +

Commercial: maximum 2 quarts per acre. Residential: 1 oz per 400 sq ft — do not exceed this. An 8 oz bottle covers approximately 3,200 sq ft. A 1-quart bottle covers a typical residential lot. A 5,000 sq ft lawn needs approximately 12–13 oz. Over-application reduces effectiveness — follow guidelines carefully.

What can I apply it on? +

Everything that grows. ArableONE™ is effective across commercial row crops, fruit trees, sugarcane, alfalfa, turf, lawns, golf courses, gardens, flowers, indoor plants, and container plants. It works on both indoor and outdoor applications in any climate.

Does it work on poor or depleted soils? +

Yes — and this is one of its most important features. ArableONE™ formulations help restore the physical, chemical, and biological vitality of depleted and underproductive soils. It also helps counteract the long-term damage that chemical fertilizers frequently cause. Dramatic improvement has been documented even in the poorest soils.

Does it contain GMOs or live microbes? +

No on both counts. ArableONE™ does not use genetically modified organisms — it is derived entirely from organic materials. It also does not contain live microbes. It works by stimulating the microbes already in your soil to proliferate naturally at a much faster rate.

Is it safe for people, animals, and marine life? +

Yes. The ArableONE™ formula is 100% natural and eco-friendly. It is safe to use around plants, animals, and marine life. It produces no chemical runoff and is suitable for use in environmentally sensitive areas.

Are there other benefits beyond yield and water? +

Yes. Produce grown with ArableONE™ shows increased shelf life, increased resistance to bruising, and improved flavor, aroma, and appearance. Post-harvest spoilage has been reduced to under 2% in documented trials. Carbon sequestration is also maximized through the optimized soil microbiome.

How do I apply it? +

Three methods: (1) Hose-end sprayer — set at 1½ teaspoons per gallon, ideal for lawns and gardens. (2) Drip or irrigation injection — mixes directly into your existing irrigation system with no modifications. (3) Broadcast spray — compatible with standard commercial equipment for large-scale applications. No special equipment required at any scale.

Is this proven? +

Yes. Documented across 38 independent field trials in real commercial environments — including sugarcane, strawberry, alfalfa, avocado, and municipal turf operations. Trials conducted by independent growers, university departments, and agro-industrial companies. Data available on request.

Why haven’t I heard of this? +

ArableONE™ is not distributed through traditional fertilizer retail channels. It’s sold direct — to commercial growers, municipalities, golf operators, and HOAs — through inquiry and direct order. The product requires proper application guidance to get results, so we work directly with buyers rather than through retail shelf placement.

Does this replace my fertilizer program? +

It significantly reduces fertilizer dependency — 80%+ reduction documented in commercial trials. Most operators don’t fully eliminate fertilizer in year one, but many reduce inputs substantially. ArableONE™ is compatible with existing fertility and IPM programs, so you can integrate it without disrupting what is already working.

How long does it last? +

One application delivers season-long performance. Once native soil biology is activated, it sustains itself through the growing season. Results typically begin within 30 days and continue through the full season. A second application is not needed or recommended within the same season.

How often do I apply it? +

Once per season. ArableONE™ is designed as a single seasonal application — not a repeat treatment program. Most users apply at the start of the growing season. Re-application within the same season is not recommended and will not improve results.
