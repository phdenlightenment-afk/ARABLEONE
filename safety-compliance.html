Testing Methodology
How These Tests
Are Conducted

Each metal was tested using Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry — the gold-standard analytical technique for trace element detection in agricultural products.

What is ICP-MS?

Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry (ICP-MS) is the most sensitive method available for detecting trace metals in complex matrices. It can detect elements at concentrations as low as parts per trillion (ppt). When a result comes back as < 0.01 ppm, it means the instrument was unable to detect the element at any concentration above its detection floor — effectively undetectable contamination.

Independent Lab

ICP-MS Precision

All samples analyzed by Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry. This method detects contaminants at sub-parts-per-billion levels — far below any regulatory threshold for agricultural inputs.
