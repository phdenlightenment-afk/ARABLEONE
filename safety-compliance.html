Testing Methodology

How These
Tests Are Run

Every metal was screened using Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry — the gold-standard analytical technique for detecting trace elements in complex matrices, capable of resolution down to parts per trillion.

What is ICP-MS?
Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry is the most sensitive method available for trace metal detection. When a result returns as < 0.01 ppm, the instrument was unable to detect the element at any measurable concentration — the metal is effectively absent. This is not simply a "low" result; it is a non-detection at the boundary of the technology's capability.
Independent Lab
Testing conducted by Qalitex Inc. at 9950 Research Drive, Irvine, CA 92618 — a third-party laboratory with no affiliation to Arable Technology. Results are unbiased and independently reproducible.
ICP-MS Precision
All nine samples analyzed by ICP-MS, detecting contaminants at sub-parts-per-billion levels — far below any applicable regulatory threshold for agricultural, soil amendment, or consumer inputs.
