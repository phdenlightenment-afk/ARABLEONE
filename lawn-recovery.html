The Science

Healthy Lawns Begin
Below the Surface

Most lawn problems are soil problems. ArableONE™ addresses the root cause — literally — by activating the native biology already in your soil.

Stronger Root Development

Activated soil microbes build a deeper, more extensive root zone — giving your lawn access to water and nutrients far below the surface. Roots that go deeper stress less.

Improved Water Efficiency

Healthier soil holds moisture longer. Documented 50% reduction in irrigation demand — the same lawn, significantly less water. Lower bills, less waste, better performance.

Better Nutrient Availability

Soil biology handles nutrient cycling naturally. Fertilizer dependency drops by 80%+ as your lawn draws what it needs through a restored microbial ecosystem.

Improved Stress Recovery

Biologically active soil bounces back faster from heat, drought, and foot traffic. Fewer bare patches, fewer repairs, more consistent appearance through the whole season.
