The Regenerative Mechanism

What Happens
Inside the Soil

The technology works in four sequential stages. Each builds on the last — which is why the results compound over an entire season and improve year after year.

1

Root Zone Activation

ArableONE™ creates a bio-stimulatory environment in the root zone that signals dormant and active soil microorganisms to begin colonizing more aggressively. Even in chemically damaged or severely depleted ground, enough microbial life exists to respond.

Works on depleted soils
2

Microbial Proliferation

Native soil bacteria and mycorrhizal fungi multiply at 2–5× their natural rate. This accelerated colonization produces a denser, more biologically active root zone — giving plants access to nutrients that were previously locked in the soil matrix.

Up to 2× root growth vs. fertilizer
3

Water & Nutrient Efficiency

Deeper roots reach soil moisture zones that surface feeders cannot. The microbial network acts as an extension of the root system — breaking down organic matter and converting nitrogen, phosphorus, and trace minerals into plant-available forms.

Up to 50% water use reduction
4

Sustained Soil Health

Unlike chemical fertilizers, which suppress or eliminate native microbial populations over time, ArableONE™ restores the biological, chemical, and physical structure of the soil. Each season's application builds on the last — improving ROI over time.

Does it work on heavily depleted or chemically damaged soil?+
Yes — and this is one of ArableONE™'s most important documented properties. Even soils with decades of heavy chemical fertilizer use retain enough native microbial life to respond. The technology has been tested on severely depleted commercial fields and still produces significant yield improvement. In some cases, depleted soils show the greatest relative gains.
Do I need to stop using my current fertilizer program?+
No. ArableONE™ is designed to work alongside existing fertility and IPM programs. Most operators reduce their fertilizer spend by 50–80% after seeing results in the first season — but you don't need to make any changes upfront. Apply it as-is and measure your own results before adjusting your program.
How soon will I see results?+
Most commercial operators report visible differences within 14–30 days — darker leaf color, stronger stem development, and improved canopy density. Full yield results are measurable at harvest. Root zone improvements can be seen by simply pulling a treated vs. untreated plant at 30–45 days.
Is it safe for organic certification programs?+
ArableONE™ contains no synthetic chemicals, GMOs, or live added microbes. It is 100% natural in formulation. For specific certification compatibility, we recommend consulting your certifying body. Contact us directly and we can provide the full ingredient disclosure needed for review.
Why only one application per season?+
Because the technology activates a self-sustaining biological process — not a temporary chemical boost that wears off. Once the microbial population is stimulated and the root zone is colonized, the biology continues to work for the full growing season without reapplication. A second application mid-season does not produce additional benefit over a single application.
Does it work on all crop types and soil conditions?+
Documented across avocados, strawberries, alfalfa, sugarcane, corn, cucumber, roses, lawns, and more — in soil types ranging from sandy loam to heavy clay, in climates from tropical Honduras to high-altitude Mexico. The mechanism is universal: every soil contains native biology, and ArableONE™ activates it regardless of crop type or geography.
